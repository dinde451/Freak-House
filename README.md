A priest enters an old forgotten house full of horrors, searching for missing children and uncovering a nightmare beyond imagination.

## **How to Play**

- **Mouse** : Move player's camera
- **Mouse Primary Button** : Shoot
- **Mouse Middle Button** : Close Notes (Chapter 1)
- **Mouse Secondary Button** : Toggle gun grip (Chapter 1), raise/lower gun (Chapters 2 and 3)


- **W** : Move forward
- **S** : Move backward
- **A** : Move to the left
- **D** : Move to the right


- **Q** : Quick Weapon Toggle (Chapter 1)
- **M** : Toggle Map
- **P** : Pause
- **Space** : Dash (Chapter 1), punch (Chapter 2)

## Contributing

Freak House is a community-driven project, and everyone is welcome to take part in its development.

You can contribute in many ways, including:

- **Artwork** — characters, environments, UI
- **Music & Sound Design** — soundtrack, SFX, ambience
- **Coding** — gameplay logic, bug fixes
- **Level / Chapter Design** — new areas, puzzles, encounters
- **Suggestions & Feedback** — balancing, mechanics, UX
- **Bug Reports** — help improve stability and performance
